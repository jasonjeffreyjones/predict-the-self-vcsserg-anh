# Predictability of Human Selves

## Prologue

This is a commissioning research brief. [Dr. Jason Jeffrey Jones](https://jasonjones.ninja) has intended to perform this research for many years. But he has not found the time. Now he hands you this brief; he is very interested to see what you produce.

## Research question

How predictable are human selves?

## Background

*Are human lives predictable?* If social science is to succeed, the answer must be yes - at least to some degree. But a realist must confront the challenge. Humans are complex individuals embedded in complex social networks and institutions. A better question is : *How predictable are human lives?*

That still leaves us with a daunting challenge. To gain traction, this research article will include an empiricial evaluation of the predictability of *human selves*. Specifically, ground truth has been provided by human individuals describing themselves at two different points in time. Respondents authored self-descriptions in response to the same prompt, with some intervening time. Predicting the content of the second response is the challenge. Sources for prediction include the first response, respondent demographics if known, world knowledge and social science theories.
